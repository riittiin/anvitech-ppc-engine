    Operator absences

    Mark an operator away for a date range. The plan skips them on those days and gives their work to someone else. Work already started stays on its machine.

      Machine maintenance

      Mark a machine out of service for a date range, for example a service or a repair. The plan runs nothing on it for those days, on either shift. If a job can also run on another machine, the plan may move it there; if it cannot, the job waits until the machine is back.

        Production analysis

        Operator efficiency for a month, worked out from Daily Entry. This is a report only. It never changes the plan.

        Efficiency is worked out for each operator's whole shift, never line by line: everything the operator made in that shift, against the shift's minutes counted once. Standard minutes earned = for every line, total actual qty (OK + rejected) × cycle time, added up. Operator minutes earned = standard minutes earned + the standard setting time on every line where a setup was typed (90 if the standard is blank) + no power + machine breakdown + tool problem + no load + other work done. No operator, and setting time beyond the standard, are not credited: they count against the operator. Operator efficiency = operator minutes earned ÷ minutes available. Overall productivity = standard minutes earned ÷ minutes available, the real output of the shift with no credits. The month figure adds up the minutes of all the operator's shifts, then divides. A shift with a line that has no cycle time, or with no minutes entered, shows "-" and is left out of the month total; its note says why. Cycle time comes from the Item's Process Master. The report starts on 03-10-2026, the first day Daily Entry had these fields. In the Excel download every figure is a formula: click a cell to see what it adds or divides, and the Working column writes the sum out in full.
